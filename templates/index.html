      How it works

      The maze is an undirected graph: every cell is a vertex and every missing wall is an edge. BFS finds the fewest steps. DFS finds some route fast. Dijkstra and A* minimise total cost on weighted tiles, and A* uses a Manhattan-distance heuristic to explore fewer cells. Greedy best-first is fast but gives no optimality guarantee.
